A journal. A stranger's story. Your own.

How it works

Connection, one page at a time. It takes about five minutes.

1

Find a journal

We leave them in third places: coffee shops, camps, waiting rooms, and more.

2

Read, then write

Flip through strangers' stories, then add an entry of your own. Always anonymous.

3

Leave it for the next person

Your words become part of a growing community, and an archive anyone can read.
