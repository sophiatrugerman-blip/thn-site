Our Partnerships

A few of the organizations we've teamed up with to bring journaling, connection, and reflection to more communities.

Citymeals on Wheels

  • Co-branded, bilingual journals in English and Spanish
  • Distributed to homebound older adults across NYC
  • Launched January 2025

NYC Book & Sip Club

  • A "Sisterhood of the Traveling Journal" — one journal passed member to member
  • Each member reflects, then passes it along to the next
  • Based in NYC, partnership started in 2025
