Our Partnerships

A few of the organizations we've teamed up with to bring journaling, connection, and reflection to more communities.

Interested in partnering with us? Get in Touch

Citymeals on Wheels

  • Co-branded, bilingual journals in English and Spanish
  • Distributed to homebound older adults across NYC
  • Launched January 2025

NYC Book & Sip Club

  • A "Sisterhood of the Traveling Journal" — one journal passed member to member
  • Each member reflects, then passes it along to the next
  • Based in NYC, partnership started in 2025

Ammirati Counseling Group

  • Journal placed in a coffee shop in Deerfield, Illinois
  • Our first journal to include guided reflection prompts, now in most of our journals
  • Partnership started in 2025

Manhattan Psychology Group

  • Five journals, each tailored to a different age group
  • Placed in their waiting area for patients and families to read and write in
  • Based in NYC, partnership started in 2025
